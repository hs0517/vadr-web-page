At VADR lab., we study the creation and mechanisms of experiences made possible by visualization environments and interactive application systems: experiences that are thrilling and exciting, and experiences that make people think, "if so, then this" or "this could be even better."
Our research addresses the nature of systems made up of people and software systems. We explore technologies for designing and implementing software systems as tools that people use, as well as visions for realizing software systems as environments that surround people.

Research Approaches

  1. Software technology: technologies for building software systems that create visual representations that change dynamically in response to human input
  2. Cognitive science: a cognitive-scientific understanding of people, such as the characteristics of perception and recognition, thinking processes, and the emergence of creativity

Our Underlying Perspective

  1. People's thinking and behavior change with the tools and environments they use
  2. People and their environments are mutually dependent
  3. As a result, the relationship between people and their environments keeps changing
