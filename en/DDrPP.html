Data Dressing and Prompted Physicality

DDrPP Data Dressing and Prompted Physicality

This research targets local historical statistical records and aims to build a data experience environment that encourages citizens' intellectual exploration and inspiration by incorporating data physicalization, one form of data representation, into the process of digitally archiving historical materials. Data physicalization refers to physical artifacts whose geometry or material properties encode data, and it is said to enable the communication of information in a way that makes full use of human perceptual abilities. Artifacts that represent data physically are also said to be effective at drawing people's interest in data. By representing data that has complex table structures and is difficult to understand, such as local historical statistical records, we create an environment in which citizens can easily engage with the history of their own region.
