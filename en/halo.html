HALO History-based Augmenting Learning Opportunities

The HALO (History-based Augmenting Learning Opportunities) project studies how people can make use of the work histories collected from their own intellectual creative activities in information environments, and what significance this has. Based on the hypothesis that the history of one's own work may serve as an indexical cue that evokes past contexts and situations, we explore the roles of work histories in human intellectual creative activities through tool prototyping and user studies. Taking programming as a concrete example of intellectual creative activity, we have been designing and implementing environments that make use of one's own work histories.
