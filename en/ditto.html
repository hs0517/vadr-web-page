DITTO Digital Instruments and Tangibles Theater Orchestrated

The DITTO (Digital Instruments and Tangibles Theater Orchestrated) project focuses on the interactions that occur between the physical world, which is governed by physical laws, and the virtual world, which is simulated through physics computation, and studies how the two interfere with each other and how users can perceive them as a coherent "reality." So far, we have been designing and implementing an Exploratorium (a set of tools for exploration) made of simple tools that let people experience the diverse ways the physical and virtual worlds act on each other, using simple physical phenomena such as lighting, shadows, mirrors, and balances as subjects.

DITTO#001: Shadows of a Virtual Cube Appearing in the Physical World

DITTO#001 creates an MR experience in which the shadow of an interactive virtual cube appears on a table in the physical world. Through the virtual cube, which users can manipulate via HoloLens 2, and video projected onto the table that follows the position of the virtual cube, users experience the illusion that light in the physical world is hitting the virtual cube and casting its shadow into the physical world.

DITTO#003: Lighting a Virtual Cube with Light from the Physical World

Building on DITTO#001, DITTO#003 creates an MR experience in which changes in the color of light from a lamp in the physical world act on the interactive virtual cube. Through a color filter that changes the color of the light and the surface color of the virtual cube, which follows the movement of the filter, users experience the illusion that light colored by the filter is illuminating the virtual cube.

DITTO#009: Changing the Color of Physical Light with a Virtual Color Filter

DITTO#009 creates an MR experience in which an interactive virtual color filter changes the color of light in the physical world. Through the virtual color filter, which users can manipulate via HoloLens 2, and projected video that follows the position of the filter, users experience the illusion that the color of light in the physical world changes at the position of the virtual color filter.

DITTO#010: Reflections of Virtual Objects in a Physical Mirror

DITTO#010 creates an MR experience in which interactive virtual objects appear to be reflected in a mirror in the physical world. Through two virtual objects that users can manipulate via HoloLens 2 and their own reflection in the mirror, users experience the illusion that the virtual objects are reflected in the physical mirror.

DITTO#011: Balancing the Weights of Physical and Virtual Objects

DITTO#011 creates an MR experience in which the weight of a virtual object acts on the physical world. Through a virtual object that users can manipulate via HoloLens 2 and a balance that tilts mechanically according to the position of the virtual object, users experience the illusion that the weight of the virtual object is acting on the balance. Physical objects can also be placed on the balance, so that the weights of virtual and physical objects are balanced against each other.

DITTO#014: Physical Shadows Cast by a Virtual Spotlight

DITTO#014 creates an MR experience in which light from a virtual spotlight operated by the user appears to illuminate a physical cube. Through the virtual spotlight, which users can manipulate via HoloLens 2, and video projected onto the table that follows the position of the spotlight, users experience the illusion that light is being cast from the virtual spotlight in the physical world.
