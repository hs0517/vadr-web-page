MAVenRead MR, AR and VR for Experiencing Novel Read

The MAVenRead project focuses on holding a book and turning its pages, one of the characteristics of reading a paper book, and aims to create new reading experiences. MAVenRead is a HoloLens 2-based system that projects digital book content onto the pages of a blank book with AR markers attached, providing an experience like reading a real book (Figure 1). Our findings suggested that reading with MAVenRead is well suited to short content with little text that can be enjoyed in a short time. Focusing on poetry as reading material that fits these characteristics, we developed MAVenReadVerse, a system for reading poems. Rather than focusing on understanding or interpreting poems, MAVenReadVerse lets readers enjoy the words of the poems themselves using MR. Building on the MAVenRead experience, we added a text animation feature so that readers can sense aspects of the words, such as their sound, that they had not noticed before (Figure 2).
